# Business Insights

## Price Distribution
The majority of listings are priced below $250, with a small number of luxury listings creating a right-skewed distribution.

## Room Types
Entire home/apartment listings dominate the market, indicating strong demand for complete properties.

## Neighbourhoods
Premium neighbourhoods command significantly higher average prices, suggesting location is a major pricing factor.

## Reviews
Most listings have high review scores, indicating generally positive guest satisfaction.

## Availability
Many listings are available for a large portion of the year, suggesting opportunities to improve occupancy.
